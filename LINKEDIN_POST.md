## Post

I have been reviving MusicSpace, an old Sony CSL idea that connects constraint propagation, spatial audio, and musical control.

The browser prototype now lets 2D sources, listeners, trajectories, and constraint nodes control sound in real time. In this short demo, moving geometric constraints drive spatialized audio loops; other patches control MIDI-like generators and synthesis parameters.

What I like about the idea is that spatialization is not drawn after the music: it becomes part of the musical structure. Geometry, constraints, motion, and sound are edited in the same space.

Live demo: https://fpachet.github.io/musicspace/
Code: https://github.com/fpachet/musicspace
