Read the geometry

Dashed lines indicate balances, solid bars fixed distance, rays radial ratios, dotted lines minimum separation, coils springs, and arcs angular constraints. Numbered badges match the inspector. Range guides show the shared bounds.

The display follows the actual engine scene, including disabled rules. Red links and an inspector warning mean a geometric relation is outside solver tolerance. Springs are intentionally elastic. An angle or distance ratio describes geometry; it does not imply the same ratio between Hz or other parameter units.

Dynamics start paused for precise exploration. Undo restores the complete geometry and constraint settings. Native preset recall keeps the stored sound and recaptures geometric targets. Technical notes
