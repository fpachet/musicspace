Why it keeps wiggling

Energy comes from the motors

Ten Faust controls form an elastic ring. Four more springs connect the ring to two moving anchors, shown as diamonds. These anchors are MusicSpace trajectories: an offset circular motion and a shuttle. Different masses, speeds and damping create a constantly changing response.

Switch the motors off to let the springs settle. Freeze motion to stop the entire simulation immediately. Audio has its own start/pause button.

The sound follows the geometry

The same real Faust synth as Study 02 supplies bass, body and upper voices, resonant filtering, tremolo and stereo pan. Distances to Orbit’s center control the sound. Faint trails show where the dots have been.

Motion starts automatically; audio starts only when you click. Background tabs pause the simulation. Undo restores the full scene and freezes it for inspection. Technical notes
